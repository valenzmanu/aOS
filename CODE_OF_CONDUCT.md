# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful, assume good faith, focus
criticism on ideas and code, and protect other people's privacy. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and publication of private data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. This policy applies in project spaces
and when representing the project elsewhere.
